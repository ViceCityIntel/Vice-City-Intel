FIELD DOSSIER / RESEARCH IN PROGRESS
01 / CITY OVERVIEW

WELCOME TO VICE CITY.

Vice City is one of the major locations featured in Grand Theft Auto VI. Inspired by the atmosphere and geography of South Florida, the city is a central part of the fictional state of Leonida.

Our objective is to document its districts, landmarks, atmosphere and points of interest as reliable information becomes available.
